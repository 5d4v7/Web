Privacy Policy

Last updated: 8 October 2026

1. Information We Collect

Depending on how you interact with Axonic, we may process Discord user ID, username/display name, server information, ticket information, messages submitted through tickets, ticket times, staff/claimer information, transcripts and information voluntarily provided through forms.

2. How We Use Information

Information may be used to provide support, process tickets, investigate bugs, handle purchase support, prevent abuse and spam, moderate the community, maintain transcripts, investigate disputes or security incidents, and improve service reliability.

3. Ticket Transcripts

Messages within tickets may be stored as transcripts. Transcripts may be posted in the designated Axonic transcript channel and sent to the ticket creator and claiming staff member, and may be accessible to authorised staff.

4. Information Sharing

We do not intentionally sell your personal information. Information may be accessible to authorised Axonic staff where necessary. Information may also be disclosed where required by law or reasonably necessary to protect Axonic, its users or others from fraud, abuse or security threats.

5. Data Retention

Ticket information and transcripts may be retained for as long as reasonably necessary for support, moderation, security, dispute resolution and record-keeping.

6. Discord

Axonic operates through Discord and may process information made available through Discord. Your use of Discord is also subject to Discord's own terms and privacy policies.

7. Your Choices

Where applicable, you may contact Axonic to ask about information associated with your tickets or request correction or deletion. Some information may need to be retained for legitimate operational, security, legal or dispute-resolution purposes.

8. Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information handled by Axonic. No online service can guarantee absolute security.

9. Changes

This Privacy Policy may be updated periodically. The latest version will be made available through official Axonic channels.

10. Contact

For privacy-related questions, contact Axonic through the official Discord server.
